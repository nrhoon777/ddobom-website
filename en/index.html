OUR MISSION

Korea’s first

What TTOBOM does

So you can focus on treatment and find your way back to daily life, we stay by your side.

  1. Changing perceptions

    From “why so young?” to assumed heredity and fragility — we push back.

  2. Emotional support

    We connect peers on the same road, so no one has to walk it alone.

  3. Better care

    We demand treatment environments and policy that fit young patients.

  4. Returning to life

    We help people focus on treatment, then find their way back to daily life.
