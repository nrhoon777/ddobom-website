OUR MISSION

Korea’s first

What Ddobom does

An organisation built for young adults with cancer.
So treatment can be the focus, and daily life can be the destination.

Changing perceptions

We break down the stigma around young adult cancer.

Emotional support

We connect peers walking the same road.

Better care

We propose treatment environments and policy that fit.

Returning to life

We help people find their way back after treatment.
