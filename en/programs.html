Ddobom Class

You come to learn. You leave with friends.

Young adults who have been through cancer gather to share what they know — a skill, a recipe, a piece of hard-won advice. Learning together is what makes the ground feel solid again.

📅 Once a month

AAM Campaign — the travel bucket list

“When I’m better, I want to go here.”
We keep that promise together.

During treatment, people write down the place they want to see when it is over. Once they come through it, Ddobom travels there with them. The journey becomes a photobook and a film — proof, for everyone else, that recovery happens.

✈️ Twice a year in Korea · once a year abroad

Cheer UP, Spring

We talk, we sing, we cry a little.

A talk-and-sing concert where survivors speak plainly about what happened to them. Putting survivors and everyone else in the same room, laughing at the same jokes, is how the stigma quietly comes apart.

🎤 4 held so far · regular since 2024

TtoTtobom TV

In the voice of someone who went first.

A YouTube channel of interviews with people who came through hardship, carrying accurate information alongside real experience. Videos are in Korean.
